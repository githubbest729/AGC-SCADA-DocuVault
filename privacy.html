Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

AGC SCADA DocuVault is an offline-first Progressive Web Application (PWA) developed for industrial automation documentation.

1. Local Data Storage

DocuVault operates entirely client-side. All documents, configurations, site notes, and embedded clipboard images are saved locally on your device via browser-based IndexedDB storage. No database records are ever uploaded to an external server.

2. External Network Requests

The application is designed to function with zero internet connectivity after its initial load. Required runtime dependencies (such as marked.js and html2pdf.js) are loaded via public CDNs (jsDelivr and Cloudflare) and safely handled offline through your browser's Service Worker cache.
